# NIST WWV Signal Mapping

Collection of predicted and field tested Signal strength mapping of NIST WWV Time Station.

## Overview

Assisting the Microwave Photonics Laboratory at Colorado School of Mines with determining whether their lab is in a deadzone from NIST's WWV Time and Frequency Station located north of Fort Collins, CO.

## Notes

- Initial assessment was conducted using Radio Mobile; a free RF propagation simulation and mapping software by Roger Coude (VE2DBE)
